Delivering your corn

Rail and truck lanes run in parallel, so trucks don't wait behind unit trains.

  1. Check in

    Scan your delivery contract or give your name at the scale.

  2. Sample and grade

    A probe pulls the load for moisture, test weight and damage.

  3. Unload

    Dump at the receiving pit and weigh out empty.

  4. Get paid

    Take your ticket now. Payment arrives in two business days.

What your corn becomes

Every bushel is used. Nothing leaves the plant as waste.

  • Fuel ethanolShipped by rail and truck to blenders across the region.
  • Distillers grainsHigh-protein livestock feed, sold wet or dried. Ask our feed desk for pricing.
  • Corn oil and CO2Corn oil goes to biodiesel, and captured CO2 goes to food and beverage use.
